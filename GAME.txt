RAY POKERI - the game and how it decides
========================================

What we have found out about the game program (8460-1.1, 10.10.1994) by
reading the ROM and by watching it run in the emulator. ROM addresses are
in hex; "A6-31152" means a variable at 0x48B00 - 31152 in RAM (the game
keeps its variables relative to register A6 = 0x48B00). "?" marks guesses.


1. Playing
----------
  PANOS (B)           bet 1-5 credits (PELIT). From bet 3 a joker is added
                      to the deck.
  JAKO (Enter)        deal five cards.
  VALITSE 1-5 (1-5)   select the cards to keep ("VALITTU" appears under
                      them). At least one card must be selected: with no
                      selection JAKO does nothing.
  JAKO                draw new cards for the others.

  After a win the bottom bar shows "VOITIT n  TUPLAATKO" (you won n, double?).
  TUPLAUS (D) starts the double-up game with PIENI / SUURI (small / big);
  that part has not been analysed. VOITONMAKSU (P) takes the win: the
  first press after a win is ignored, the second moves it onto the VOITOT
  counter. VOITONMAKSU then pays VOITOT out as coins from the hopper.

  Pay table, bet 1 (higher bets multiply it):
    VIITOSET      five of a kind (only with the joker)    -
    VARISUORA     straight flush                          20
    NELOSET       four of a kind                          15
    TAYSKASI      full house                              10
    VARI          flush                                    5
    SUORA         straight                                 4
    KOLMOSET      three of a kind                          3
    KAKSI PARIA   two pairs                                3
  One pair does not win.

  With no credits and nobody playing, the attract mode starts after about
  80 seconds: "POKERI (c) RAY 1990" with bouncing cards, the pay table and
  "PUDOTA RAHA" (insert coin).


2. Machine information (service test TESTI 2)
---------------------------------------------
Door open (F10), K16 (F6), then VOITONMAKSU twice:

    8460-1.1      the game, built MON OCT 10 16:10:03 EET 1994
    6458-7.7      the system part, linked TUE SEP 27 10:49:39 EET 1994,
                  by "KJL" on "SUNRAY9", in /USR2/LINK/J1
    42463242      checksum?

The game is the EPROM in IC43; the system part is IC30, IC38 and IC34
(see HARDWARE.txt). The system part also contains a network protocol for
a central computer ("vltCommProtocol", VLT = video lottery terminal, with
Motorola/Intel byte order conversion). The emulator doesn't connect it;
the game runs stand-alone.


3. Random numbers
-----------------
One 32-bit number, kept in the battery-backed bookkeeping area (record
offset 252), is stepped 100 times a second (ROM 0x1A9D8):

    x = x * 65537 + K           (K = A6-28548)

65537 is a weak multiplier: the low 16 bits just count up by K. Because
it runs continuously, the cards you get depend mostly on the exact moment
you press JAKO.


4. Dealing
----------
ROM 0x18F6C deals the free card positions. It turns one 32-bit random
number into five cards: if the number is below the largest multiple of
N^5 that fits in 32 bits (N = cards in the deck, the constants at 0x18F8E
are 11*52^5, 10*53^5, 9*54^5, 8*55^5) it is read as five base-N digits,
otherwise a new number is drawn (at most 10 tries). This "rejection
sampling" makes every card equally likely: a careful design.

If a card is already in use, the code tries three other values derived
from the seed and then steps to the next free card. That last step makes
a card that follows an already used card very slightly more likely; a
small bias, nothing more.

Every dealt card is marked with the current round number in a "used"
table (A6-31060). The round number goes up at the end of each draw.

The first deal is not interfered with.


5. After the draw: taking wins back
-----------------------------------
This is the interesting part (ROM 0x1917A-0x194CE). After the draw the
game rates the hand (ROM 0x17C84: 2 = two pairs, 3 = three of a kind,
4 = straight, 6 = full house, others not checked). Then, for

    two pairs, three of a kind, and a straight

it takes one random byte d (0-255) and computes

    threshold = table[bet][hand] + correction

If the threshold is bigger than d, it throws away one of the cards that
make the win and deals a new one, and repeats this until the hand no
longer qualifies. The card thrown away is always one the player just drew,
never a held card, and it all happens before the cards are shown. The
player cannot see it: the card simply never appears, although it stays
marked in the "used" table (that is how the emulator counts take-backs).

The table (game parameters, ROM 0x30052 + 3246):

    bet   two pairs   three of a kind   straight
     1       28            29              0
     2       17            16              0
     3       73            73             73
     4       60            60             60
     5       46            46             46

So at bet 1, with no correction, 28/256 = 11 % of two-pair draws are
taken back; at bet 3 it is 73/256 = 28 %. Straights have 0 at bets 1-2,
so there they are only taken back when the correction is positive. The high values from bet 3
balance the joker, which makes wins much more frequent.

The emulator's bottom panel shows the live values.


6. The correction: a payout controller
--------------------------------------
The game computes its running payout percentage from the books
(ROM 0xD63A):

    won  = bets - money in + money out + credits + pending wins + [206]
    pct  = won * 10000 / bets          (0 when nothing has been bet)

and from it the correction (ROM 0x18E28, values from the parameters):

    payout          correction
    over 94 %          +13
    93-94 %            +10
    92-93 %             +8
    91-92 %             +5
    90-91 %             +3
    80-90 %              0
    79-80 %             -3
    78-79 %             -5
    77-78 %             -8
    76-77 %            -10
    under 76 %         -13

Paying too much makes it take more wins back, paying too little makes it
more generous. The machine steers itself to 80-90 %. A new machine (no
bets yet) starts at the generous end, -13.


7. Settings in the game's parameter block
-----------------------------------------
The block at ROM 0x30052 (pointer at A6-29534) holds the game's settings.
What we know of it:

    offset   value   meaning
     856       1     joker in use
    2360       0     other take-back mode: instead of the table, hands
                     rated 8 or more (probably straight flush and five of
                     a kind) are taken back with 50 % chance, if 3304 is
                     non-zero. Off in this ROM.
    3240       3     lowest bet level with the joker
    3246..     table of section 5 (three words per bet level)
    3276..   13 10 8 5 3        corrections above 90 % (section 6)
    3286..   -13 -10 -8 -5 -3   corrections below 80 %
    3304       0     see 2360

Other versions of the game (other regions, the English one) probably
differ in this block.


8. Bookkeeping ("kassamuisti")
------------------------------
Kept three times in battery RAM (pointers at A6-31532/-31528/-31524),
in 18-byte records (16 bytes + CRC-16); ROM 0xDC70 recomputes a record's
CRC and copies it to the other copies. Known fields (byte offsets):

      0   money held = [12] + [18] + [22]
      4   money in (coins)
      8   money out (paid)
     12   payout float: coins in the hopper for paying out
    108   total bets
    116   PELIT (credits)
    120   VOITOT (wins waiting to be paid)
    126   closure code (word): low byte FF = open, e.g. 0x1384 = P3 84
     26   coins moved from hopper 1 to the cash box
    130   hopper fills (an attendant filling the hopper): a fill adds
          to [12], [0] and [130] (ROM 0xB538)
    162   hopper 1 level (word), set in TESTI 1 "HOPPERIN 1 RAJA". When
          the float is more than 50 coins above it, the game moves the
          excess to the cash box, up to 50 coins at a time (ROM 0xC3D4,
          coin unit command 25 n). With level 0 it keeps unloading.
    252   the random number of section 3

At start the game checks (ROM 0xB27C)

    [0] = [12] + [18] + [22]
    [4] + [130] - [144] - [0] - [8] - [26] - ... - [102] = 0

and closes with "P3 00 kassavika" if they don't hold. It also closes
with "P3 84" (hoppers empty) and "P3 89" (payout reserve too small: the
float must cover the biggest possible win). F9 in the emulator refills
the float and clears these.

The start-up self test also checksums the EPROMs: a modified ROM makes the
machine close itself (closure code 0EE0).


9. Not looked at yet
--------------------
- The double-up game (TUPLAUS, PIENI/SUURI): it may have its own logic.
- The attract mode's demo hands: that code (ROM 0x1BEAE, case 3 at
  0x1BF00) deals up to four times until it gets at least two pairs,
  probably so that the demo shows a win. It is not used in real play.
- Fields 18, 22, 144, 206 and most of the parameter block.

The emulator sets the hopper 1 level to the fill amount (1000) when it
fills the hopper, like an attendant would.
