RAY POKERI - the game and how it decides
========================================

What we have found out about the game program (8460-1.1, 10.10.1994) by
reading the ROM and by watching it run in the emulator. ROM addresses are
in hex; "A6-31152" means a variable at 0x48B00 - 31152 in RAM (the game
keeps its variables relative to register A6 = 0x48B00). "?" marks guesses.


1. Playing
----------
  PANOS (B)           bet 1-5 credits (PELIT). From bet 3 a joker is added
                      to the deck.
  JAKO (Enter)        deal five cards.
  VALITSE 1-5 (1-5)   select the cards to keep ("VALITTU" appears under
                      them). At least one card must be selected: with no
                      selection JAKO does nothing.
  JAKO                draw new cards for the others.

  After a win the bottom bar shows "VOITIT n  TUPLAATKO" (you won n, double?).
  TUPLAUS (D) starts the double-up game with PIENI / SUURI (small / big),
  see section 6. VOITONMAKSU (P) takes the win: the first press after a
  win is ignored, the second moves it onto the VOITOT counter. VOITONMAKSU
  then pays VOITOT out as coins from the hopper.

  Pay table (credits won, parameter block offset 534, one row per bet).
  It is not a plain multiple of bet 1:

    bet  KAKSI   KOLMO-  SUORA  VARI   TAYS-  NELO-  VARI-  VIITO-
         PARIA   SET                   KASI   SET    SUORA  SET
     1     3       3       4      5     10     15     20      -
     2     6       6       8     10     20     30     40      -
     3     6       6      12     15     30     45     60     60
     4     8       8      16     20     40     60     80     80
     5    10      10      20     25     50     75    100    100

    KAKSI PARIA two pairs, KOLMOSET three of a kind, SUORA straight,
    VARI flush, TAYSKASI full house, NELOSET four of a kind,
    VARISUORA straight flush, VIITOSET five of a kind (only with the
    joker). One pair does not win.

  From bet 3 two pairs and three of a kind pay 2 per credit instead of 3:
  together with the higher take-back rates (section 5) this pays for the
  joker.

  With no credits and nobody playing, the attract mode starts after about
  80 seconds: "POKERI (c) RAY 1990" with bouncing cards, the pay table and
  "PUDOTA RAHA" (insert coin).


2. Machine information (service test TESTI 2)
---------------------------------------------
Door open (F10), K16 (F6), then VOITONMAKSU twice:

    8460-1.1      the game, built MON OCT 10 16:10:03 EET 1994
    6458-7.7      the system part, linked TUE SEP 27 10:49:39 EET 1994,
                  by "KJL" on "SUNRAY9", in /USR2/LINK/J1
    42463242      checksum?

The game is the EPROM in IC43; the system part is IC30, IC38 and IC34
(see HARDWARE.txt). The system part also contains a network protocol for
a central computer ("vltCommProtocol", VLT = video lottery terminal, with
Motorola/Intel byte order conversion, see section 10). The emulator doesn't connect it;
the game runs stand-alone.


3. Random numbers
-----------------
One 32-bit number, kept in the battery-backed bookkeeping area (record
offset 252), is stepped 100 times a second (ROM 0x1A9D8):

    x = x * 65537 + K           (K = A6-28548)

65537 is a weak multiplier: the low 16 bits just count up by K. Because
it runs continuously, the cards you get depend mostly on the exact moment
you press JAKO.


4. Dealing
----------
ROM 0x18F6C deals the free card positions. It turns one 32-bit random
number into five cards: if the number is below the largest multiple of
N^5 that fits in 32 bits (N = cards in the deck, the constants at 0x18F8E
are 11*52^5, 10*53^5, 9*54^5, 8*55^5) it is read as five base-N digits,
otherwise a new number is drawn (at most 10 tries). This "rejection
sampling" makes every card equally likely: a careful design.

If a card is already in use, the code tries three other values derived
from the seed and then steps to the next free card. That last step makes
a card that follows an already used card very slightly more likely; a
small bias, nothing more.

Every dealt card is marked with the current round number in a "used"
table (A6-31060). The round number goes up at the end of each draw.

The first deal is not interfered with.


5. After the draw: taking wins back
-----------------------------------
This is the interesting part (ROM 0x1917A-0x194CE). After the draw the
game rates the hand (ROM 0x17C84: 2 = two pairs, 3 = three of a kind,
4 = straight, 6 = full house, others not checked). Then, for

    two pairs, three of a kind, and a straight

it takes one random byte d (0-255) and computes

    threshold = table[bet][hand] + correction

If the threshold is bigger than d, it throws away one of the cards that
make the win and deals a new one, and repeats this until the hand no
longer qualifies. The card thrown away is always one the player just drew,
never a held card, and it all happens before the cards are shown. The
player cannot see it: the card simply never appears, although it stays
marked in the "used" table (that is how the emulator counts take-backs).

The table (game parameters, ROM 0x30052 + 3246):

    bet   two pairs   three of a kind   straight
     1       28            29              0
     2       17            16              0
     3       73            73             73
     4       60            60             60
     5       46            46             46

So at bet 1, with no correction, 28/256 = 11 % of two-pair draws are
taken back; at bet 3 it is 73/256 = 28 %. Straights have 0 at bets 1-2,
so there they are only taken back when the correction is positive. The high values from bet 3
balance the joker, which makes wins much more frequent.

The emulator's bottom panel shows the live values.


6. The double-up (TUPLAUS)
--------------------------
ROM 0x18380; checked by playing it in the emulator.

  TUPLAUS        doubles the stake on offer (the first press only shows
                 TUPLAATKO, like VOITONMAKSU).
  PIENI / SUURI  choose small / big (ROM 0x1A944 passes 1, 0x18ED8
                 passes 2).

Only after the choice is one card dealt, by the same dealer and random
number as the poker game (section 4), from a fresh full deck. Nothing
looks at the choice, the books or the payout percentage: there is no
take-back here.

    small   2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and the ace
    big     8, 9, 10, J, Q, K
    7       loses, whatever you chose
    joker   wins (only in the deck from bet 3)

So 24 of 52 cards win (46.2 %): on average a double returns 92.3 % of the
stake. With the joker it is 25 of 53 (47.2 %), 94.3 %. Every double costs
the player about 6-8 % on average; it is fair in the sense that it is not
rigged, but not even.

Doubling is offered only while the win is at most 50 (parameter 3306).
When a won double goes over 50 the doubling ends, and the random number
modulo 14 picks a message (ROM 0x1850A):

    1   SINA KYNIT MINUT!    you fleeced me!
    5   MISTA TIESIT!        how did you know!
    7   USKOMATONTA          unbelievable
    9   OLEN IHMEISSANI!     I'm amazed!
   10   KOETAS UUDELLEEN!    try again!
   12   LUOVUTAN!            I give up!
   13   ONNEKAS ARVAUS!      lucky guess!
  other  the developers' initials, in three lines:
            EL  JT   ML  TP
            RM  PJP  KP  SL  TT  LM
            KJL JOLA OH  KK  MZ  ES

So half of the time the machine shows a hidden credits screen. KJL is
also the name in the link stamp shown in TESTI 2. (Read from the code;
not yet seen in the emulator.)


7. The correction: a payout controller
--------------------------------------
The game computes its running payout percentage from the books
(ROM 0xD63A):

    won  = bets - money in + money out + credits + pending wins + [206]
    pct  = won * 10000 / bets          (0 when nothing has been bet)

and from it the correction (ROM 0x18E28, values from the parameters):

    payout          correction
    over 94 %          +13
    93-94 %            +10
    92-93 %             +8
    91-92 %             +5
    90-91 %             +3
    80-90 %              0
    79-80 %             -3
    78-79 %             -5
    77-78 %             -8
    76-77 %            -10
    under 76 %         -13

Paying too much makes it take more wins back, paying too little makes it
more generous. The machine steers itself to 80-90 %. A new machine (no
bets yet) starts at the generous end, -13.


8. Settings in the game's parameter block
-----------------------------------------
The block at ROM 0x30052 (pointer at A6-29534) holds the game's settings.
What we know of it ("?" = guess):

    offset   value   meaning
     150..   bit masks for the buttons and lamps (two lists, 150-262
             and 266-342) ?
     382..   a table of number triples (60 1 60, 80 2 80, 100 3 100,
             ... 3000 40 3000), maybe the game pricing ?
     534..   the pay table of section 1: 8 words per bet level
             (two pairs ... five of a kind), rows 64 bytes apart
     856       1     joker in use
     986..   the screen texts: 244 pointers (relative to 0x30000) to
             every text the game shows, from "OVI AUKI" (door open)
             and the service menu to the fault names and the messages
             of section 6. A version in another language would mostly
             differ here.
    2270..   16 colour words (a grey ramp and some colours) ?
    2360       0     other take-back mode: instead of the table, hands
                     rated 8 or more (probably straight flush and five of
                     a kind) are taken back with 50 % chance, if 3304 is
                     non-zero. Off in this ROM.
    3216..   1 5 10 ...         coin and note values ?
    3240       3     lowest bet level with the joker
    3246..     table of section 5 (three words per bet level)
    3276..   13 10 8 5 3        corrections above 90 % (section 7)
    3286..   -13 -10 -8 -5 -3   corrections below 80 %
    3304       0     see 2360
    3306      50     largest win that may be doubled (section 6)

Other versions of the game (other regions, the English one) probably
differ in this block.


9. Bookkeeping ("kassamuisti")
------------------------------
Kept three times in battery RAM (pointers at A6-31532/-31528/-31524),
in 18-byte records (16 bytes + CRC-16); ROM 0xDC70 recomputes a record's
CRC and copies it to the other copies. Known fields (byte offsets):

      0   money held = [12] + [18] + [22]
      4   money in (coins)
      8   money out (paid)
     12   payout float: coins in the hopper for paying out
    108   total bets
    116   PELIT (credits)
    120   VOITOT (wins waiting to be paid)
    126   closure code (word): low byte FF = open, e.g. 0x1384 = P3 84
     26   coins moved from hopper 1 to the cash box
    130   hopper fills (an attendant filling the hopper): a fill adds
          to [12], [0] and [130] (ROM 0xB538)
    162   hopper 1 level (word), set in TESTI 1 "HOPPERIN 1 RAJA". When
          the float is more than 50 coins above it, the game moves the
          excess to the cash box, up to 50 coins at a time (ROM 0xC3D4,
          coin unit command 25 n). With level 0 it keeps unloading.
    202   game features (long), shown and set in TESTI 14
          "PELIOMINAISUUDET" as 8 hex digits, see below
    252   the random number of section 3

The features word (from the manual; 1 = on). Not used in the poker game:
bits 0, 1, 3, 4, 5, 8, 11, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 31.

     6   pay credits: credits are paid out too on VOITONMAKSU
     7   routing error: close the machine after three bad routings (G18)
     9   exchange: pay notes straight out as coins (change machine)
    10   immediate pay: pay wins out automatically
    12   coin count: counters show coins (scaled by the smallest coin)
    13   win music: play the jackpot tune for a big win. In this ROM it
         plays for a win of 50 or more (parameter 3306, ROM 0x1A7DE);
         the manual says "more than half the top win".
    18   return credits: give back credits and wins after a fault is reset
    19   no punish: don't clear credits and wins on faults or tilts
         (except a too-long hopper pulse)
    21   test coins: pay test coins in TESTI 0
    22   calibrate hoppers: the counted hopper contents become the counter
    23   test coins: pay test coins during cash collection
    24   drop coin transfer: hopper to cash box (only models with a coin
         diverter after the hoppers)
    25   tilt sensor: close the machine if it is tilted too far
    26   hand pay all: jackpots paid fully by hand (normally only the
         hundreds are)
    27   partial pay: pay out automatically only the part over the
         forced hand-pay limit
    28   block notes: note acceptor off during payout
    29   saver off: don't dim the attract screen (ROM 0x188D6, 0x1AEFA:
         brightness 63 instead of 59/35)
    30   network: connected to a casino network (section 10)

Example from the manual: immediate pay = bit 10 = 00000400. A new machine
has 00000000. The emulator sets 20002000 (win music and saver off) when it
creates the battery RAM.

The game has 16 sounds (table at A6-31440, filled from the parameter
block); the code plays them by number (ROM 0xE19E). Sound 1 is the win
music. Sound 53 plays sound (books byte 135) & 7; what sets that byte is not known yet.

At start the game checks (ROM 0xB27C)

    [0] = [12] + [18] + [22]
    [4] + [130] - [144] - [0] - [8] - [26] - ... - [102] = 0

and closes with "P3 00 kassavika" if they don't hold. It also closes
with "P3 84" (hoppers empty) and "P3 89" (payout reserve too small: the
float must cover the biggest possible win). F9 in the emulator refills
the float and clears these.

The start-up self test also checksums the EPROMs: a modified ROM makes the
machine close itself (closure code 0EE0).


10. The central computer link (vltCommProtocol)
-----------------------------------------------
The system part (ROM 0x6000-0x9600) contains a complete serial protocol
for a central computer. VLT = video lottery terminal, the networked
gaming machines of the time. A packet is

    EA  type  sequence  length  data (at most 150 bytes)  check1 check2  0D

Inside a packet the bytes EA, F3 and 0D are sent with an F3 in front of
them. The two check bytes come from two running sums over the packet
(ROM 0x8D3C). The packet types are CONNECT_REQ, CONNECTION_CHECK, ACK,
NAK and MSG; the receiver complains about BAD_CHECKSUM and LONG_PACKET,
and about message counters that don't match. The code converts numbers
between Motorola and Intel byte order, so the other end was probably a
PC.

What it was for, guessing from the texts: reading the books and alarms
from a distance and controlling cash collection. Texts like "PC windows
ei vastaa" (PC Windows not responding), "Hairio tietoliikenteessa"
(data link disturbed), "Rahastusta ei sallittu" (cash collection not
allowed), "Sahkolukko jumissa" (electric lock stuck) and log lines like
":Aut:vip;ovi ;syy ;pvm ;klo" (machine, door, reason, date, time) point
that way. It probably uses the optional DUART chip, which the emulator
doesn't have, so the game runs stand-alone.


11. Not looked at yet
---------------------
- What the base return of the poker game is (before take-backs).
- The attract mode's demo hands: that code (ROM 0x1BEAE, case 3 at
  0x1BF00) deals up to four times until it gets at least two pairs,
  probably so that the demo shows a win. It is not used in real play.
- Fields 18, 22, 144, 206 and most of the parameter block.

The emulator sets the hopper 1 level to the fill amount (1000) when it
fills the hopper, like an attendant would.
